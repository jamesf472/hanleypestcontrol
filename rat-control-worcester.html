Signs You May Have Rats

  • Scratching noises in walls or loft spaces
  • Rat droppings around the property
  • Gnaw marks on wood, plastic or cables
  • Greasy smear marks on walls
  • Burrows around gardens and buildings
  • Damage to stored food or packaging
